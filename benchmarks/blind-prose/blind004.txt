The Boomer Esiason Foundation (BEF) is a non-profit organization that was established in 1993 by former NFL quarterback Boomer Esiason, shortly after his son Gunnar was diagnosed with cystic fibrosis. The foundation is dedicated to helping people with CF and their families. Among other things, it funds research for a cure and treatments for those with CF, and provides scholarships and other support for people with the disease.

If you would like to get involved with supporting the BEF, there are a number of ways you can do so. You can donate directly to the foundation, either online or by mail. You can also participate in events that the foundation hosts, such as the Empire Challenge football game, the BEF Golf Classic, or the BEF Tailgate. You can volunteer your time to help with fundraising events, or participate in one of the foundation's awareness campaigns, such as National CF Awareness Month in May. Additionally, you can purchase merchandise from the foundation's online store, or spread the word about the foundation and its mission on social media.
