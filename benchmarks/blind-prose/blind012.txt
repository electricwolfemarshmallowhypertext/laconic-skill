Absolutely! Here are some ideas for other recipes you could use the lavender and orange compound butter in:

1. Scones - Spread the compound butter on fresh scones for a lovely breakfast or snack.

2. Biscuits - Serve the compound butter with hot, fresh biscuits for a delicious twist on a classic side dish.

3. Grilled chicken - Melt the compound butter and brush it on grilled chicken for a unique and flavorful entree.

4. Roasted vegetables - Toss roasted vegetables with a little bit of the melted compound butter for a flavorful side dish.

5. French toast - Use the compound butter instead of regular butter for a delicious and decadent French toast breakfast.

6. Pancakes - Spread the compound butter on top of a stack of fluffy pancakes for a delicious breakfast treat.

7. Sweet potatoes - Melt the compound butter and toss it with roasted sweet potatoes for a savory and sweet side dish.

8. Salad dressing - Use the compound butter as a base for a unique salad dressing. Mix it with a little vinegar and olive oil for a flavorful dressing that's perfect for any salad.

The possibilities are endless with this flavorful compound butter!
