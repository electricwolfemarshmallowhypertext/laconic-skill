The 2019 Audi Q5 is expected to see improvements in fuel efficiency thanks to upgrades in the drivetrain and other features. While the exact gas mileage has not yet been confirmed, industry experts expect the Q5 to achieve around 30 mpg on the highway. This is an improvement over the previous model, which achieved 25 mpg on the highway. The Q5's 2.0-liter TFSI engine with 4 cylinders will remain the only option for the US market, but Audi is expected to enhance its gas economy with various systems and features. Overall, the Q5 is likely to be a fuel-efficient SUV that is competitive with other models in its class.
