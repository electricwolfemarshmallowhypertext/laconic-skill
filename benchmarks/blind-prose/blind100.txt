Classic Homemade Potato Soup Recipe

Ingredients
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 6 medium potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Optional toppings: shredded cheddar cheese, chopped scallions, crumbled bacon

Instructions
1. Start by preparing the vegetables. Peel and chop the potatoes and set aside. Chop the onion and garlic cloves and set aside.

2. In a large pot or Dutch oven, melt 4 tablespoons of unsalted butter over medium heat until melted.

3. Once the butter has melted, add the chopped onion to the pot and cook until the onion is soft and translucent. This should take around 5-7 minutes.

4. Add the minced garlic to the pot and cook for another minute or until fragrant.

5. Pour 4 cups of chicken or vegetable broth into the pot and bring it to a boil.

6. Add the chopped potatoes to the pot and stir to combine. Lower the heat to medium-low and let the soup simmer for 15-20 minutes or until the potatoes are tender.

7. Once the potatoes are tender, turn off the heat and let the soup cool for a few minutes.

8. Using an immersion blender or a regular blender, blend the soup until smooth.

9. Turn the heat back on to medium-low and add 1 cup of milk and 1/2 cup of heavy cream to the pot. Stir well to combine.

10. Let the soup simmer for a few more minutes until it has thickened slightly.

11. Taste the soup and add salt and pepper to your liking.

12. Serve the soup hot with your preferred toppings such as shredded cheddar cheese, chopped scallions, or crumbled bacon.

Variations/Substitutions:
- For a vegan version of the soup, substitute the butter with olive oil, the chicken/vegetable broth with vegetable broth, the milk with almond milk, and the heavy cream with coconut cream.
- For a healthier version of the soup, substitute the heavy cream with Greek yogurt or sour cream.
- To add more flavor to the soup, try adding chopped carrots, celery, or bacon to the pot while cooking.
