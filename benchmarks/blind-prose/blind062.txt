foreshadowing is an important literary device that authors use to build tension and anticipation in their works. It involves giving subtle hints or clues about what may happen in the future; thereby, preparing readers for what's to come.

by including foreshadowing throughout the book, the author creates a sense of anticipation in the reader's mind. They keep reading to uncover what's going to happen and how the events leading up to it relate to the foreshadowing.

this technique also helps to create a sense of foreboding or suspense, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat. Foreshadowing allows the author to create a sense of intrigue and tension in the reader's mind, making the overall impact of the story more powerful.

in summary, the use of foreshadowing can contribute significantly to the overall impact of a story by building tension and suspense, creating anticipation in the reader's mind, and adding depth and complexity to the plot.
