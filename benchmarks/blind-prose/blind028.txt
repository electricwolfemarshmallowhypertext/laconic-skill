Swansea managed to earn their first ever point this season after conceding the first goal. Tottenham had looked strong, with Van der Vaart scoring just before half-time, but a period of heavy pressure from Swansea led to Scott Sinclair scoring the equalizer in the 84th minute. This setback means that Tottenham is now six points behind league leaders Manchester City and Manchester United, with the gap likely to increase to nine if leaders beat Sunderland on Sunday. It was a frustrating result for Tottenham, who had looked like they might close the gap at the top of the table.
