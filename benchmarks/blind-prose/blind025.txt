The Agreement on National Indigenous Housing Information is an agreement between the Australian Federal Government, State and Territory Governments, and a number of Indigenous and non-Indigenous organisations. The purpose of the agreement is to address the lack of reliable and up-to-date information about housing conditions for Indigenous people in Australia, and to develop a national framework to improve the provision of such information. The agreement aims to improve the knowledge and evidence base necessary to support policy and program development, and to support better decision-making and monitoring of progress in relation to Indigenous housing. The National Indigenous Housing Information Implementation Committee was established to oversee the implementation of the agreement.
