Of course! One podcast I would recommend is titled "A Great-Granddaughter's Introduction," which features a high school student interviewing her great-grandmother about her life experiences. In the podcast, the great-grandmother shares stories about growing up during the Great Depression, working as a schoolteacher, and even meeting her future husband at a USO dance during World War II. It's a touching and personal conversation that reveals a lot about family history and the resilience of the human spirit. You can find the podcast on the StoryCorps website or on podcast platforms such as Spotify or Apple Podcasts.
