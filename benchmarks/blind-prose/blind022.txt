The 2019 Audi Q5 will feature a plug-in hybrid drivetrain to improve its performance and reduce emissions, making it a more environmentally friendly SUV. While diesel may be an option in other countries, the hybrid combo will likely be the only powertrain option in the US due to emissions concerns. The exterior design will see some cosmetic upgrades to the bumper and grille, but overall the vehicle will maintain its dimensions and shape. Expect the new Audi Q5 to debut in the summer of 2018 with three trim levels: Premium, Premium Plus, and Prestige. Pricing for the entry-level model is expected to start around $45,000, with the Prestige model potentially reaching $60,000 when fully loaded with features.
