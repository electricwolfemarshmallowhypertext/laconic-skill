Pete Blieberg is the senior game coordinator for the Empire Challenge football game, which is a full-time job and he has been involved with the game for 20 years. His responsibilities include scheduling practice sites and times, coordinating the coaching staffs, sizing players for uniforms, and setting up physicals. He has been involved with every Heroes Bowl since its inception in 1996, and only seven people have been involved with every game. Blieberg's sons have grown up with the game, starting as water boys and graduating to equipment managers, and this year they designed the uniforms. The game is a charity event that has raised more than $2 million for cystic fibrosis and helped launch 36 NFL careers. Blieberg's favorite memory was Gunnar Esiason starting at quarterback in 2009, a boy with cystic fibrosis who handed off to a speedy player for a touchdown that set the tone in Long Island's win. Blieberg is an essential member of the team and has done a great job with everything else.
