Yes, some of the key industries that are driving Seoul's economy include:

1. Technology: Seoul is home to many leading tech companies like Samsung, LG, and SK Telecom. These companies play a major role in driving innovation and growth in the city's economy.

2. Finance: Seoul also has a strong financial sector, with many banks, insurance companies, and investment firms located in the city. The Seoul Stock Exchange is a major financial market in the region.

3. Tourism: Seoul is a popular tourist destination, attracting millions of visitors each year. The city's rich history, culture, and entertainment industries contribute significantly to its tourism sector.

4. Retail: Seoul has a thriving retail industry with many large shopping malls and markets, such as Myeong-dong and Dongdaemun. The city is also home to many international brands and luxury boutiques.

5. Automotive: The automotive industry is also a significant contributor to Seoul's economy. Hyundai and Kia, two of the largest automobile manufacturers in South Korea, are based in the nearby city of Ulsan but have a strong presence in Seoul.

These industries, along with many others, help make Seoul one of the most dynamic and diversified economies in South Korea.
