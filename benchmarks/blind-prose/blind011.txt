To make the compound butter with lavender and orange zest, start by crushing the lavender in a mortar and pestle, or by rubbing it between your fingers until it is fragrant. Add the crushed lavender and orange zest to the softened butter in a medium mixing bowl and mix thoroughly. Use a wooden spoon to break up the butter and mix until it is smooth and the flavors are evenly distributed.

Next, turn the butter out onto a long piece of wax or parchment paper and use the paper to flatten and shape the butter into a long rectangular shape. Make sure the butter is evenly distributed and not too thick. Cover the butter with one edge of the paper and begin rolling tightly into a log shape. Twist each end closed as you would a piece of candy to seal it up.

Place the compound butter log into the refrigerator or freezer to set up for several hours or overnight. Once chilled and firm, you can slice the butter into rounds and use it to top all sorts of baked goods like shortbread, muffins, pancakes, and even roast chicken.

The combination of fragrant lavender and bright orange zest in this compound butter adds a unique flavor that complements sweet and savory dishes alike. It's easy to make and can be stored in the fridge or freezer for whenever you want to add a touch of extra flavor to your meals or baked goods.
