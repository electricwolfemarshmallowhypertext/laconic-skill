I would recommend the Strawberry & Pomegranate cocktail as it is packed with vitamins, fiber, and microelements. Here is the recipe:

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup fresh strawberries, mashed
- 1/2 cup pomegranate juice
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1 tsp honey
- Ice
- Fresh mint leaves, for garnish

Instructions:
1. In a cocktail shaker, combine the mashed strawberries, pomegranate juice, orange juice, honey, and ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass.
3. Garnish with fresh mint leaves and enjoy!

This cocktail is not only delicious but also healthy and refreshing. Enjoy!
