The COVID-19 pandemic has had a significant impact on Seoul's economy and the industries driving it. The measures taken to contain the virus, such as social distancing, travel restrictions, and business closures, have resulted in a sharp decline in economic activity.

The tourism industry has been hit particularly hard, with many travel restrictions in place and the cancellation of events and conferences. Many hotels and tour operators have seen a sharp decline in bookings and revenue.

The technology industry, on the other hand, has seen a spike in demand, as more people are working and studying from home. However, production and supply chains have been disrupted, causing delays and price fluctuations.

The government has taken various measures to address the economic impact of the pandemic, including fiscal stimulus packages, low-interest loans, and tax relief for small businesses. The city has also launched several programs to support affected industries, such as the Seoul Digital New Deal, which aims to boost the city's digital infrastructure and promote innovation.

Seoul has also launched a "Rent Emergency Support" program for small business owners who are struggling to pay rent due to COVID-19. The city is also working to promote domestic tourism and offer incentives to encourage people to visit local attractions.

Overall, while the pandemic has had a significant impact on Seoul's economy, the city and the government are taking steps to mitigate the impact and support recovery.
