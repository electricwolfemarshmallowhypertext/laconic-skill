1. Green Tea Margarita
- 1 1/2 oz tequila
- 1 oz fresh green tea
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 1/2 oz agave nectar
- Ice
- Salt, for rimming (optional)

Instructions:
1. Rim a glass with salt (optional).
2. In a cocktail shaker, combine the tequila, green tea, lime juice, agave nectar, and ice.
3. Shake well and strain into the rimmed glass.
4. Garnish with a lime wedge and enjoy!

This cocktail is loaded with antioxidants and is a perfect way to relax and unwind after a long day.

2. Kiwi Coconut Mojito
- 1 oz white rum
- 1/2 kiwi, peeled and mashed
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 1 oz coconut water
- 1 oz club soda
- Fresh mint leaves
- Ice

Instructions:
1. In a cocktail shaker, combine the white rum, mashed kiwi, lime juice, and ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass.
3. Add the coconut water and club soda and stir.
4. Garnish with fresh mint leaves and enjoy!

This cocktail is a delicious twist on the classic mojito and is packed with vitamin C and electrolytes.

3. Beet & Berry Lemonade
- 1 oz vodka
- 1/2 cup fresh beet juice
- 1/2 cup fresh mixed berry juice
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1 oz honey
- Ice

Instructions:
1. In a cocktail shaker, combine the vodka, beet juice, mixed berry juice, lemon juice, honey, and ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass.
3. Garnish with a lemon slice and enjoy!

This cocktail is loaded with antioxidants and is a great way to incorporate more fruits and veggies into your diet.

Enjoy!
