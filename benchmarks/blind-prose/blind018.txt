As Jack journeys through the ruins of the Mojave Wasteland, he finds himself in a tight spot in the Sierra Madre Casino in the Dead Money DLC. With no way out and trapped by the casino's deadly security system, Jack teams up with a group of fellow survivors to take on the casino's mastermind and escape with the casino's riches.

After narrowly escaping the Sierra Madre, Jack continues his journey through the Mojave Wasteland in Chapter 4. This time, he's tasked with assisting a group of NCR soldiers in securing a nearby bunker that holds valuable technology. Along the way, Jack must navigate complex political alliances and fend off numerous enemies, all while striving to complete his mission and bring home the advanced technology that could turn the tide of the war.

Finally, with his mission complete and a wealth of new gear and resources at his disposal, Jack takes on the Old World Blues DLC. Here, he finds himself in a strange and mysterious location where he must uncover the secrets of a long-lost science project gone awry. With a host of new challenges and enemies to contend with, Jack must use all his skills and resources to successfully navigate this dangerous and unpredictable landscape and emerge victorious.
