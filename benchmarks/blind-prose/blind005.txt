To boost engagement and gather more customer data, email marketers can incorporate interactive elements such as countdown timers, integrated forms, and video clips into their emails. These technologies are predicted to catch on in email marketing in the coming years, and can help marketers deliver personalized messages, streamline the sales funnel, and turn recipients into brand advocates. Interactive elements like countdown timers and integrated forms can generate a sense of urgency and increase conversions, while video clips can provide engaging content that showcases products or explains how to use an app. Gathering customer data through creative polls and surveys can help marketers craft more personalized emails, while analytics software like Intercom can help them make actionable findings based on customer touch points. And with the advent of AI and machine learning, the future of email marketing may involve tailored campaigns that are optimized by algorithms rather than human marketers.
