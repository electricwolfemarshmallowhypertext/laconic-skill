As she stumbled through the maze, the darkness pressing in on her from all sides, she couldn't shake the feeling of being followed. Every time she turned around, there was nothing there, but she could feel eyes on her, cold and malevolent.

She started to run, her heart racing with fear, but the footsteps behind her kept pace, getting closer and closer with each passing second. She risked a glance behind her and saw a shadowy figure darting through the maze towards her, its eyes glowing in the darkness.

She ran faster, her breath coming in short gasps, but the creature was getting closer. She could feel its breath on her neck, could hear its low growls as it closed in for the kill. She knew that she had to do something, anything, to shake it off.

She turned a corner and saw a dimly lit room up ahead, with a door that looked like it led outside. She ran towards it, hoping against hope that it wasn't a trap.

She burst through the door and found herself outside, safe in the light of the moon. But as she turned to look back at the maze, she saw the creature's eyes gleaming in the darkness, its claws scraping against the door.

She knew that it would only be a matter of time before it found a way out, and she had to keep moving if she wanted to stay ahead of it.

She ran through the forest, her heart pounding in her chest, her eyes scanning the darkness for any signs of the creature. She heard rustling in the bushes and saw shadows darting through the trees, but every time she looked closer, there was nothing there.

And then, suddenly, she saw it. It was standing there in front of her, its eyes fixed on her, its claws extended. She tried to run, but it was too late. The creature was on top of her, its jaws closing around her neck, its glowing eyes the last thing she saw before everything faded to black.

She woke up in a cold sweat, her heart racing with fear. It had only been a nightmare, but it felt so real, so vivid. She knew that it would haunt her for a long time to come, reminding her of the horrors that lurked just beyond the edge of the light.
