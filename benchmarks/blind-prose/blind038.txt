Yes, several other religions use lunar calendars. For example:

1. Judaism: The Jewish calendar is based on the lunar cycle and is used to determine the dates of Jewish holidays, such as Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, and Passover.

2. Hinduism: The Hindu calendar is also a lunar calendar used in India and Nepal to determine the dates of Hindu festivals and religious ceremonies.

3. Buddhism: Some Buddhist traditions, especially in Southeast Asia, use lunar calendars to determine important dates, such as full moon days.

4. Chinese traditional religion: The Chinese lunar calendar is used in Chinese traditional religion to determine the dates of festivals such as the Chinese New Year.

Overall, lunar calendars are more closely tied to nature and the cycles of the moon, which often play an important role in religious observations and celebrations.
