Certainly! The Empire Challenge football game is a charity event that raises money for cystic fibrosis. Over the past 20 years, the game has attracted more than 250,000 fans and raised more than $2 million for the disease. Cystic fibrosis is a genetic disorder that affects the respiratory, digestive and reproductive systems, and there is currently no cure. The game helps to fund research into treatments for cystic fibrosis and raise awareness of the condition. In addition to the football game, there are various fundraising activities that take place, such as a VIP party before the game. The money raised from the event goes to benefit the Boomer Esiason Foundation, which works to fund research to find a cure for cystic fibrosis and improve the quality of life for people with the disease.
