I can tell you that whether or not to use foreshadowing and to what extent is ultimately a creative choice for writers. While effective use of foreshadowing can greatly enhance a story, there may be instances where it is not necessary or appropriate.

some stories may rely more on other literary devices such as symbolism, metaphor, or imagery, and may not require much foreshadowing. Others may follow a more straightforward narrative path, where foreshadowing may not serve the intended effect.

ultimately, each author has the creative freedom to decide how best to weave foreshadowing into their story, taking into account the genre, tone, and pacing of their work. However, foreshadowing, if used thoughtfully and effectively, can add depth and complexity to a story and keep readers engaged and intrigued.
