# Laconic Anti-Slop Reference

Use this as the style spec. The verifier enforces deterministic subsets of these rules.

## Remove announcement openings

Start with the answer, not a runway.

- "Sure"
- "Of course"
- "Absolutely"
- "Here's the thing"
- "Here's why"
- "Here's what"
- "Let me explain"
- "Let me be clear"
- "To answer your question"
- "I'd be happy to help"

## Remove filler emphasis

These phrases perform confidence instead of adding information.

- "Make no mistake"
- "Let that sink in"
- "This matters because"
- "Full stop"
- "The reality is"
- "It's worth noting"
- "At its core"

## Remove process narration

The user does not need the model to narrate its answer structure.

- "Let me walk you through"
- "The rest of this"
- "As we'll see"
- "I want to explore"
- "Moving forward"

## Avoid vague business phrasing

Prefer concrete actions over business fog.

- "Deep dive"
- "Lean into"
- "Circle back"
- "Take a step back"
- "On the same page"
- "In today's landscape"
- "When it comes to"

## Preserve meaning

Do not shorten by deleting facts the user needs. If trimming would remove the answer, fail instead of pretending the rewrite fixed it.
