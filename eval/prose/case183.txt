Sure! Here are some specific steps on how to arrange the coals for indirect grilling using charcoal:

1. First, light your charcoal in a chimney starter until the coals are glowing red and covered in white ash.

2. While the coals are lighting, clean the grates of your grill with a grill brush to remove any leftover debris from previous cooking sessions.

3. Once your coals are ready, use tongs to transfer them to one side of the grill. Create a pile of hot coals on one side, leaving the other half of the grill empty.

4. Place a drip pan on the empty side of the grill. The drip pan should be placed right in the center of the empty side of the grill.

5. Once you have your coals arranged on one side and your drip pan on the other side, it's time to put your meat on the grill! Place your meat directly above the empty side of the grill, so it's not directly over the hot coals.

6. Close the lid of your grill and adjust the vents to regulate the temperature. For indirect grilling, you should aim to maintain a temperature of around 225-250 degrees Fahrenheit.

7. During the cooking process, try to avoid lifting the lid too often as this will reduce the temperature and slow down the cooking process.

8. For best results, use a meat thermometer to check the internal temperature of your meat as it cooks. Once your meat has reached its desired temperature, remove it from the grill and let it rest for 15-20 minutes before slicing and serving.
