Certainly, here are some examples of literary works that convey the theme of unconditional love and support:

1. "The Giving Tree" by Shel Silverstein - This children's book tells the story of a tree that gives all it has to a boy who grows up and returns to the tree throughout his life, showing that true love means giving without expectation of receiving anything in return. 2. "The Color Purple" by Alice Walker - This novel portrays the strong bond between two sisters, Celie and Nettie, who stay connected despite being separated for many years. The story reveals how love and support can transcend distance and hardship. 3. "A Thousand Splendid Suns" by Khaled Hosseini - This novel depicts the enduring connection between two women, Mariam and Laila, who are married to the same abusive husband. Their love and support for each other help them survive the oppressive conditions they face. 4. "To Kill a Mockingbird" by Harper Lee - This novel explores the relationship between a father and daughter, Atticus and Scout Finch. Atticus provides unwavering love and support to his daughter throughout her experiences of growing up and learning about the injustices of the world. 5. "The Sound and the Fury" by William Faulkner - This novel portrays the complicated family dynamics and shows how even when faced with challenges, the family members stick together and support one another. These literary works show how love and support can come in different forms, but all emphasize the importance of the unconditional bond between individuals.
