As for pairing this cashew cheese with a drink, here are a few suggestions:

- White wine, such as Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Grigio, can be a good choice because it's refreshing and doesn't overpower the tangy flavors of the cheese. - A dry cider or beer, like an IPA or a Belgian Tripel, can also be a great match because it's carbonated and can cut through the richness of the cheese. - If you prefer non-alcoholic options, you might try serving the cheese with herbal tea or sparkling water with a wedge of lemon or lime for added flavor.
