Wil Wheaton played the role of Joey Trotta in the 1991 action thriller film Toy Soldiers. The film follows a group of boys at a boarding school who are taken hostage by terrorists seeking the release of a drug lord. Wheaton's character, Joey, is one of the students caught up in the situation and helps lead the resistance against the terrorists.

Joey Trotta is portrayed as a rebellious and outspoken student who often gets into trouble. However, he proves to be courageous and resourceful in the face of danger. Wheaton's performance in the film was praised for adding depth and complexity to his character.

Toy Soldiers also starred Sean Astin, Keith Coogan, and Denholm Elliott. While it was not a commercial success, the film has gained a cult following and is remembered as a thrilling and entertaining action movie.
