Sure! Here's a recipe for Southwest-Style Grilled Beef Tri-Tip that would work well for indirect grilling:

Ingredients:
- 2-3 lb beef tri-tip
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Juice of 1 lime
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro

Instructions:
1. In a small bowl, mix together the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Rub the spice mixture all over the beef tri-tip.

2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the lime juice, olive oil, onion, garlic, and cilantro. Pour the mixture over the beef, making sure it's evenly coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or up to overnight.

3. Preheat your grill for indirect grilling. If using a gas grill, heat one side of the grill to medium-high heat and leave the other side off. If using a charcoal grill, arrange the coals on one side of the grill to create a direct heat and leave the other side empty for indirect heat.

4. Once your grill is ready for indirect grilling, place the beef tri-tip on the cooler side of the grill, fat side up, and close the lid. Cook for 30 minutes to 1 hour, or until the internal temperature of the beef reaches your desired doneness.

5. Once the beef tri-tip is finished cooking, transfer it to a cutting board and let it rest for 15-20 minutes before slicing it against the grain. Serve with your favorite sides and enjoy!

This dish has a flavorful southwestern twist that will be sure to impress your family and friends at your next BBQ or dinner party.
