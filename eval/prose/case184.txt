Sure! Here's a recipe for Beer Braised Beef Brisket that would work well for indirect grilling using either a charcoal or gas grill:

Ingredients:
- 3 lbs beef brisket
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 bottle of beer
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced

Instructions:
1. Start by seasoning the beef brisket with salt and pepper. In a small bowl, mix together the smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and chili powder, then rub the spice mixture all over the brisket.

2. Preheat your grill for indirect grilling. If using a gas grill, heat one side of the grill to medium-high heat and leave the other side off. If using a charcoal grill, arrange the coals on one side of the grill to create a direct heat and leave the other side empty for indirect heat.

3. Once your grill is ready for indirect grilling, place the brisket on the cooler side of the grill, fat side up, and close the lid. Cook for 3-4 hours, or until the internal temperature of the brisket reaches 195-200Â°F.

4. Meanwhile, in a large Dutch oven, combine the beer, beef broth, onion, and garlic. Bring to a boil and then reduce the heat and let it simmer for about 2-3 hours, or until the liquid has reduced by half.

5. Once the brisket has finished cooking, transfer it to the Dutch oven with the beer mixture. Cover and let it simmer over low heat for another hour or until the meat is fall-apart tender.

6. Remove the brisket from the Dutch oven and let it rest for 15-20 minutes before slicing it against the grain. Serve with the beer sauce and enjoy!

This dish is perfect for feeding a crowd and is a great option for a summer BBQ or special occasion dinner.
