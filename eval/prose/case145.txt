Certainly! Here are some variations to try:

1. Strawberry and Almond Butter Pudding: Instead of blueberry compote, use strawberry compote and swap the almond butter for peanut butter.
2. Chocolate and Almond Butter Pudding: Add 1-2 tablespoons of cacao powder to the pudding mixture and top with chopped almonds and chocolate chips.
3. Mango and Coconut Pudding: Use coconut milk instead of almond milk and add diced mango to the pudding mixture before refrigerating.
4. Raspberry and Vanilla Pudding: Use raspberry compote and add a teaspoon of vanilla extract to the pudding mixture before refrigerating.
5. Banana and Cinnamon Pudding: Mash a ripe banana and mix it into the pudding mixture along with a sprinkle of cinnamon before refrigerating. Top with sliced bananas and granola.

Enjoy experimenting with these variations!
