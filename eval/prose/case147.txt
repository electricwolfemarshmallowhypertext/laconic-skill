Yes, there are many delicious nut butter options that you can use in this chia pudding recipe. Here are some alternatives to almond butter:

1. Peanut butter: This is a classic option that pairs well with jam and is very creamy.

2. Cashew butter: Cashew butter has a mild flavor and pairs well with many fruits and flavors.

3. Hazelnut butter: If you love the taste of Nutella, you'll love hazelnut butter. It has a rich and nutty flavor that pairs well with chocolate and berries.

4. Pecan butter: Pecan butter is slightly sweeter than almond butter and has a unique flavor that pairs well with maple or pumpkin spices.

5. Macadamia nut butter: This butter has a buttery and creamy taste and is perfect for adding richness to the pudding.

Any of these nut butters add a different flavor dimension to your chia pudding. So feel free to experiment with them and see which one you like the best.
